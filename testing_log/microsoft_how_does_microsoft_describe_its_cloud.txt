QUESTION
--------
How does Microsoft describe its cloud strategy?

ANSWER
------
Microsoft describes its cloud strategy as building the Intelligent Cloud and Intelligent Edge Platform, which involves investing in high-performance and sustainable computing to meet the growing demand for fast access to Microsoft services. The company's cloud business benefits from three economies of scale: 

1. Datacenters that deploy computational resources at a lower cost per unit
2. Coordinating and aggregating diverse customer demand patterns to improve resource utilization
3. Multi-tenancy locations that lower application maintenance labor costs.

Additionally, Microsoft's cloud strategy includes powering OpenAI's workloads as its exclusive cloud provider, developing specialized supercomputing systems, and offering a hybrid infrastructure with integrated security, compliance, identity, and management capabilities. The company also provides industry clouds with customized capabilities and a consistent multi-cloud and on-premises management platform through Azure Arc.
